What this book is about

A field manual for living in the Arkansas woods. You will learn which plants are safe to eat, how to move through the woods without getting lost, how to build a simple shelter, and how to stay safe without panicking.

Chapter 1: Move through the woods safely

Always tell someone where you are going. Carry more water than you think you need. Wear boots and long pants. Stay on clear paths when you can, and mark your route if you are going far.

Chapter 2: Plants you can safely use

In the Ozark woods, three plants matter most: - Blackberries: ripe berries are safe and good to eat. - Cattails: the young stalks and roots can be eaten in spring and early summer. - Dandelion: leaves and flowers are edible, use them in salad or tea.

Never eat a plant you cannot identify with a second source. A single mistake can make you very ill.

Chapter 3: Build a simple lean-to shelter

  1. Find a fallen tree or a sturdy lean of branches against a tree trunk.
  2. Lean smaller branches and leaves against it to block the wind and rain.
  3. Put your gear in one corner.
  4. Keep the opening away from the wind.

Chapter 4: Make a fire safely

Collect dry wood and clear a space down to bare soil. Keep water nearby. Never leave a fire unattended. Put it out completely before you sleep.

Safety warning

Do not touch or eat anything you cannot name. Always carry a light and a map. Let someone know where you are going. When in doubt, stay out of the deep woods alone.
