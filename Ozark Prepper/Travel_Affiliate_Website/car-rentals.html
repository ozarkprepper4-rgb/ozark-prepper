Where to rent a car on a budget

Compare more than one agency before you book. A one-week rental usually costs less per day than a one-day rental.

Budget

Often the cheaper national agency. budget.com

Avis, Enterprise, Hertz

National chains with many locations. Compare their prices on the same dates — they differ.

Local and independent rental agencies

Smaller agencies can be cheaper for a short trip. Check reviews and the exact pick-up address first.

What to check before you book: young-driver fee, mileage limits, fuel policy, insurance and whether your own car insurance covers rentals, and the drop-off location and time.
