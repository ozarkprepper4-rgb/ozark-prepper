How to Find Cheap Flights with a Plain-English Guide

This guide shows, in plain words, how to find cheap flights. No jargon. No confusing math. Just steps.

What you need

  • A computer or phone with internet
  • A free flight search site (Google Flights is free)
  • A few minutes

Step 1. Enter where and when

Type your start and end. Pick your dates.

Step 2. Search many dates

Try dates one day before and one day after. Small date changes often cut the price.

Step 3. Sort by price

Look at the list from lowest to highest. Pick a result and read it.

Step 4. Compare the total price

Look at the final price, not the first number. Add bags and seats.

Step 5. Check the airline rules

Read the carry-on, seat, and change rules. Cheap does not always mean good.

Step 6. Use a price alert

Set an alert so you know when the price drops.

Step 7. Book when the price is right

Book when the price feels fair. Do not wait for a big drop.
