What this book is about

A 5-gallon bucket filter is a simple way to make cloudy river or rainwater clearer and safer to drink. It is not a full treatment system. You still need to boil or add a clean disinfectant such as unscented chlorine bleach. This book shows you the build and the limits.

Chapter 1: What you need

  • One clean 5-gallon food-grade plastic bucket with a lid
  • A spigot that fits the bucket
  • Clean cotton or a coffee filter
  • Sand, fine and coarse
  • Gravel
  • A clean cloth
  • A small tube or pipe for the spigot

Chapter 2: Build the filter

  1. Drill a hole near the bottom of the bucket for the spigot. Make sure the hole is clean and the spigot seals well.
  2. Place cotton or a coffee filter over the bottom hole. This holds the sand and gravel in.
  3. Add a layer of fine sand, then coarse sand, then gravel, in layers.
  4. Cover with a clean cloth.
  5. Fill the bucket with the water you want to filter.

Chapter 3: Use it with care

Run only the clearest water through it, then boil that water for one full minute (or three minutes at high altitude). Let the boiled water cool before you drink it. The filter clears cloudiness so it can be treated more easily.

Chapter 4: Keep it clean

Never drink from a filter you are not sure about. Wash the bucket with soap and water between uses. Replace the sand and gravel if they look dirty. Label the filter so nobody else drinks from it by mistake.

Safety warning

A DIY filter removes cloudiness, not every germ. Always boil or disinfect the filtered water before you drink it. Do not use this filter for water that looks oily, chemical-smelling, or from a known sewage spill. When in doubt, do not drink it.
