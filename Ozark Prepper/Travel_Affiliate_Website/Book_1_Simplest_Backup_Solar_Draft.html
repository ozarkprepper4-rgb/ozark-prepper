Chapter 1: Why a small solar backup is the smartest first step

If the power goes out, you should know what to do before it happens. You do not need a huge system to start. You need one small, listed backup power station.

Chapter 2: What you actually need in dollars and parts

  • 100-200Wh portable power station
  • 2x 100W foldable solar panels
  • A gentle mount and the correct cables First setup cost: $250-$450. We used free tools to price it out; no purchases were made by the agent.

Chapter 3: How to wire it safely (plain words)

  1. Pick an outdoor power outlet you can reach in the dark.
  2. Plug the power station into the outlet.
  3. Connect your devices to the power station USB and aux ports.
  4. Lay the panel in the sun, angle the legs, plug the panel into the station.
  5. Wait for the battery icon to turn green before relying on it.

Safety warning (must print on cover of final book)

NEVER modify commercial power cables. Use only listed, UL-listed equipment. When in doubt, stop and ask a licensed electrician.
