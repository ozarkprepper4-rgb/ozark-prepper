Where to buy travel gear

You do not need to buy anything to travel. If you need a bag, a flashlight, or a first-aid kit, buy from a place that ships well and has a real return policy.

Amazon

Wide choice and fast shipping if you already have an account. amazon.com

Walmart / Target

Everyday stores, good for cheap gear you can pick up the same day.

Local hardware store and thrift store

A bag, a water bottle, and a first-aid kit cost little second-hand. Check that zippers and seams work before you go.

What to buy first: a good backpack that fits under the seat, a pack of wet wipes, and a small first-aid kit. These cost little and save you on every trip.
