Where to buy travel gear

You do not need to buy anything to travel. But if you need a bag, a flashlight, or a first-aid kit, the best place for you is the one that ships well, has a real return policy, and does not mark up obscure items.

Amazon (no tax/bank upfront)

https://www.amazon.com/gp/browse.html?node=123456789&ref=ozarkprepper

— no tax/bank upfront

If you already have an Amazon account, you can add a bag, a flashlight, or a first-aid kit to your cart with your email and credit card. Amazon handles tax at checkout.

Walmart / Target — no tax/bank upfront

Everyday boxes and local stores. Good for cheap, immediately usable gear.

What to buy first: a good backpack (fits under the seat), a pack of wet wipes, and a small first-aid kit. These cost little and save you on every trip.
