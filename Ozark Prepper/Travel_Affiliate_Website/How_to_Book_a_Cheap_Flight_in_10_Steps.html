How to Book a Cheap Flight in 10 Plain Steps

Book a cheap flight without the stress. These steps use free tools and take about 30 minutes. Read them first, then book.

Step 1. Pick where you want to go

Choose your start and end. Pick a flexible date if you can.

Step 2. Search more than one site

Use a free site like Google Flights. It shows many airlines at once.

Step 3. Try to fly mid-week

Tuesday and Wednesday are often cheaper than Friday or Sunday.

Step 4. Fly at dawn or late night

These seats are often cheaper. Morning flights can cost less than afternoon.

Step 5. Set a price alert

Many free sites let you set an alert. You get an email when the price drops.

Step 6. Check carry-on rules

Cheap airlines often charge for your bag. Bring only a personal item if you can.

Step 7. Check seat choices and changes

Some seats cost money. Some tickets cannot be changed for free.

Step 8. Read the small print

Check the change fee and the refund rule. Read the airline’s terms.

Step 9. Book with a paid card only if it gives free protection

A credit card can add travel protection for free. Use a secure site.

Step 10. Keep your booking

Save your booking number, the airline, and the fare rules. Keep them in a safe place.
