Where to book: what actually fits

Almost every hotel chain will take a booking with your email and a credit card. The ones that ask for tax or bank numbers up front are usually bigger chains with automatic tax handling — those are the ones I mark needs-tax-info below.

Booking.com (no tax/bank upfront)

https://www.booking.com?pid=ozarkprepper

— no tax/bank upfront

The widest choice. Takes an email + credit card, and pulls the local tax at checkout. You do not give your Social Security number or bank account here. https://www.booking.com

Cheap Lodging Network — no tax/bank upfront

Discount motels and inns from your email. Good for weeknight stays.

Super 8 / Holiday Inn Express — needs-tax-info

National chains. You start with your email, but the system asks for your tax or bank details automatically. If you do not want to give that, pick the first two links instead.

Affiliate links on this page: Booking.com is a clearly labelled partner link — I may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

How to book cheap: look up dates on Google Flights / Skyscanner and compare lists, then click through to the hotel page. Weeknight and mid-week stays are cheaper. Read the cancellation policy before you put in your card.
