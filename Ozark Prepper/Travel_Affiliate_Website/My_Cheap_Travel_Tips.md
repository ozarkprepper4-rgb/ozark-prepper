# My Cheap Travel Tips: What I Learned from Flying Cheap

I have flown cheap for most of my life. I do not fly first class. We use free
tools. These are the things that saved me money. I share them so you can do the
same.

## Tip 1. Pack light
Most cheap airlines charge for bags. If you bring only a personal item, you
save money on every trip. A small backpack fits under the seat.

## Tip 2. Fly mid-week
Tuesday and Wednesday are often cheaper than Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.

## Tip 3. Check the small print
Read the carry-on size and weight. Read the change fee. Read the refund rule.
Cheap is not cheap if you pay more to change it.

## Tip 4. Use free tools
Google Flights, Expedia, and airline sites let you compare prices free.
Do not pay a travel agent to do this.

## Tip 5. Set a price alert
A price alert tells you when the price drops. You get an email for free.

## Tip 6. Be flexible with dates
Flying one day before or after can save you money.

## Tip 7. Choose less popular airports
Small airports often have cheaper flights than big ones.

## My honest pick for you
If you want the least stress with one bag free: **Southwest**.
If you want the absolute lowest price and you travel with only a personal
item: **Frontier** or **Allegiant**, and pack light.
