Refund Policy

14-day full refund

If an ebook doesn't work for you — the file won't open, or it's not what the description promised — you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

How it works: refunds are handled through Stripe, the payment provider. Use the receipt you received at checkout. We do not monitor the email inbox, so we cannot take refund requests there.

How fast: Stripe returns the money to the card you paid with; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Digital goods

Our products are downloadable ebooks. When a refund is approved, please stop using the files. You keep everything you paid for up to that point — we don't charge you twice for the same book.

Affiliate purchases

For purchases made through affiliate links (Amazon, Signature Solar, travel partners), the refund is handled by that store under its own policy. We do not monitor email, so contact the store directly.
