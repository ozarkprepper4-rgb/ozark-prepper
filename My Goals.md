# My Goals

A comprehensive blueprint of Neil's strategic goals, prioritizing physical preservation and total self-reliance.

## 1. Urgent & Immediate Goals (Health & Revenue)
* **Goal:** Eliminate the need for physical/manual labor.
* **Timeline:** Immediate (High Urgency).
* **Strategy:** Rapidly monetize the [[Ozark Prepper]] brand using free platforms, affiliate setups, and digital products (Phase 1 of [[Plans]]).
* **Guardrails:** Keep business and personal finances strictly segregated. Always build credit strategically and find the absolute cheapest rates.

## 2. Technical Independence (Local AI)
* **Goal:** A sovereign, offline-capable AI command platform.
* **Execution:** Designed and activated [[HavenOS]] on a local home server on Linux Mint, powered by [[Ollama]] and orchestrated by [[Destiny]].
* **Key Feature:** All data remains local, secure, and private (no paid cloud locks).

## 3. Financial Expansion (Asset Building)
* **Goal:** Deploy and grow [[NW Investments]] (Phase 2 of [[Plans]]) once steady cash flow is achieved from Phase 1.
* **Strategy:** Real estate acquisition, leasing, and capital management. Let [[Destiny]] monitor properties and market alerts 24/7.

## 4. The Sanctuary (Physical Independence)
* **Goal:** Construct a fully automated, connected [[Off-Grid Bunker and Homestead]].
* **The Vision:** A secure sanctuary with specialized facilities (medical/animal clinics, solar microgrid, fabrication shops) overseen by [[HavenOS]].
