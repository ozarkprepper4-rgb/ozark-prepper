What the shop owner gets

  • The assistant answers common questions any hour — no more voicemail tag.
  • It books estimate visits and service calls straight into a list the owner checks each morning.
  • It only says what the owner approves — it cannot invent prices or promises.
  • Every conversation is logged, so nothing gets lost.
