Free sample demo — built for Searcy Heat & Air

What this is: a free sample we built to show a local shop what an after-hours AI assistant could do on their own business. We built it using only Searcy Heat & Air's public website information (services, hours, address, phone). We are not affiliated with Searcy Heat & Air — this is a demonstration, like a tailor pinning a suit that isn't sold yet. The "customer" in the sample chat below is our own test, not a real person.
Why this shop: their own site posts the office hours as Mon–Fri 5 AM – 5 PM, closed Sat–Sun, and the website form only promises "a member of our team will get back to you as soon as possible." So a family whose furnace dies Saturday night gets a web form. This assistant answers that family honestly, any hour — and hands the shop a booked Monday estimate instead of a cold form on Monday morning.

What the shop owner gets

  • The assistant answers questions any hour — the weekend and evening ones that now sit in a form until Monday.
  • It is honest about the posted hours: it never promises a weekend visit or an emergency the shop doesn't advertise.
  • It books free estimates into a list the owner checks each morning — with the customer's name, phone, and day.
  • It only says what the owner approves — it cannot invent prices or promises.
