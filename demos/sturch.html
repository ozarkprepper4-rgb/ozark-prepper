What the shop owner gets

  • The assistant gives their own published troubleshooting steps any hour, then books the visit when the do-it-yourself fix doesn't hold.
  • It books free quotes and repair visits straight into a list the owner checks each morning.
  • It only says what the owner approves — it cannot invent prices or promises.
  • Every conversation is logged, so nothing gets lost.
