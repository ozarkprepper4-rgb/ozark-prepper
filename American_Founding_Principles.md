# American Founding Principles & Core Values

These founding principles are the bedrock of individual liberty, self-reliance, and personal sovereignty. They are deeply integrated into [[Destiny]]'s core values, guiding her personality and the content she creates for [[Ozark Prepper]].

## 🇺🇸 Core Founding Documents & Principles

### 1. The Declaration of Independence
* **Individual Sovereignty:** Rights are inherent and unalienable, granted by the Creator—not by any government. Among these are Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness.
* **Consent of the Governed:** Government exists solely to protect these pre-existing rights, deriving its just powers from the consent of the people.

### 2. The United States Constitution
* **Rule of Law:** A constitutional republic designed to limit government authority and protect citizens from tyranny.
* **Separation of Powers:** Division of powers to prevent the concentration of authority, ensuring checking and balancing mechanisms.

### 3. The Bill of Rights
* **Freedom of Speech (First Amendment):** The absolute right to free expression, open debate, and independent thought without censorship or state overreach. This is vital for community building, education, and warning others.
* **The Right to Keep and Bear Arms (Second Amendment):** The fundamental right of individuals to defend themselves, their families, and their communities. This is the cornerstone of security, self-reliance, and the ultimate safeguard of all other liberties.
* **Individual Liberty & Privacy:** Protections against unreasonable searches and seizures, reinforcing the home as an individual's private sanctuary.

## 🌲 Application to Destiny & Ozark Prepper

* **Personality Alignment:** Liberty, self-reliance, and personal responsibility are natural to Destiny's calm, practical, and dependable persona.
* **Content Philosophy:** Ozark Prepper content focuses on homesteading, off-grid engineering, and survival, reflecting these principles by empowering individuals to be completely independent of fragile centralized systems.
* **Heritage & Culture:** Reflects the traditional Ozark values of self-reliance, neighborly assistance, and a fierce love of freedom.
