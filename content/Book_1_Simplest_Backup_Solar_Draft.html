The Simplest Backup Solar Setup for Beginners

Free guide — part of the Ozark Prepper library

This is a free, plain-English guide to building a small backup solar system. No jargon. No expensive gear. No electrician required to start.

If the power goes out, you should know what to do before it happens. You do not need a huge system to start. You need one small, listed backup power station. This book is about the simplest backup solar setup you can build. It is cheap, it is safe, and it is easy to learn.

What you will need (first time, about $400–$500)

  • A small power station (200 Wh or more) — $250
  • A 100W foldable solar panel — $150
  • Cables and a small stand — $50

Where to put it

Choose a dry, cool place. Keep it away from kids and pets. Keep it off the floor if the floor gets wet. A shelf, a cabinet, or a box works.

Safety first: NEVER modify commercial power cables. Use only listed, UL-listed equipment. When in doubt, stop and ask a licensed electrician.
