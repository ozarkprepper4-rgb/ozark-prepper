Book 2: The 10-Step Cheap Flight Booking System

Follow these 10 steps to lock in the cheapest flight every time. Written so anyone can follow them.

  1. Search on Skyscanner or Google Flights
  2. Pick the whole-month view to spot the cheapest days
  3. Set a price alert on the airline's site
  4. Pick a flexible date if your schedule allows
  5. Check the airport — a smaller nearby airport can save you money
  6. Fly mid-week (Tue–Thu) instead of Fri–Sun
  7. Pack carry-on only to avoid checked-bag fees
  8. Read the bag fees before you buy
  9. Use points or miles if you have them
  10. Book with your credit card points for the best value
