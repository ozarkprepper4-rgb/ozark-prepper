Starting Preps on a Tight Budget

Free guide — part of the Ozark Prepper library

You do not need money to start preparing. You need to notice what you already have. Look around your home. The things you already own can become part of your plan.

This guide shows you how to start with what you have, then add a little at a time without going into debt.

The cheapest first steps

  1. Write down what you already own: food, water, tools, clothes.
  2. Find one thing that could fail and plan one way to handle it.
  3. Spend the least amount of money first, only on what is missing.

Free ways to prepare

  • Walk your home and list things you already have.
  • Check your local library for free information.
  • Talk to neighbours about sharing skills and tools.

Safety first: Never spend money on gear you do not understand. If you buy anything, buy used first and check it works.
