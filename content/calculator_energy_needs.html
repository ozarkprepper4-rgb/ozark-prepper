🔌 Safety Notes

  • Fuse/breaker: Size every fuse or breaker at 125% of the continuous load, then round up. This follows standard electrical code and prevents overheating.
  • Wire gauge: Use the wire gauge table based on your wire run length (in feet) and amp rating. Longer runs need larger wire.
  • Battery safety: Lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries need ventilation. Never store them in a sealed box. Keep them away from direct heat.
  • Grounding: All metal parts and the battery must be grounded to prevent shock.
  • Panel mounts: Mount panels where they will not be struck by vehicles or trampled.

How to use
