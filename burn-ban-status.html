Dry outside? Read this before you burn.

  • Check first, light second. A burn ban means no outdoor burning — brush piles, trash, leaves, chimineas, all of it.
  • Keep water right there. A charged hose or full buckets beat good intentions.
  • Wind is the killer. If it's breezy, don't burn even when it's legal.
  • Never leave a fire. Not for a minute. Stir the coals and feel them with the back of your hand.
