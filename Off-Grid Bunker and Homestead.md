# Off-Grid Bunker and Homestead

The ultimate physical destination: a fully automated, sovereign, self-sufficient homestead compound located in "the middle of nowhere."

## 🌲 The Vision (The Sanctuary)
To build a physical sanctuary that ensures the safety, comfort, and independence of Neil, his loved ones, and their animals, fully integrated with and automated by [[HavenOS]].

## 🏗️ Compound Infrastructure Layout
* **Main Dwelling / Bunker:** Deeply insulated, secure primary living structure with robust physical security parameters.
* **Fabrication Workshop:** Housing heavy tooling, 3D printers, woodworking benches, and automated metal/plasma/CNC milling machines.
* **Off-Grid Solar Shed:** Complete LiFePO4 battery array and solar inverter grid designed by [[Sustenance]] to provide continuous power without utility support.
* **Wood & Metal Shop:** Fully ventilated workshop for physical fabrication and building homestead structures.
* **Homestead Medical Clinic:** A well-stocked, private first-responder room with diagnostic tools, medical logs, and medication inventory.
* **Animal Clinic:** A designated area for veterinary care and medical support for homestead livestock and pets.

## 🛰️ Technological & Resource Integrations
* **Inference Command:** Host servers running offline [[Ollama]] models so that [[Destiny]] and her sub-agents can automate security, garden irrigation, and water pumps.
* **Food & Stockpiles:** High-density shelving tracked by [[Provident]] to estimate precise days-of-survival margins.
* **Radio Comms Hub:** Active high-frequency antennas designed by [[Comms]] to establish links to emergency HAM networks during blackouts.
* **Active Agriculture:** Greenhouse and poultry logs managed by [[Sustenance]].
