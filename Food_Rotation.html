Food Rotation System

How to store food, rotate stock, and maintain a sustainable food supply.

📦 Stock Rotation

FIFO (First In, First Out) method. Use oldest food first. Label with dates.

📅 Rotation Schedule

Check expiration dates monthly. Replace expiring items. Keep 3-month supply minimum.

🌡️ Temperature

Store food at 50-70°F. Cool, dark place. Avoid humidity and direct sunlight.

📦 Containers

Mylar bags, food-grade buckets, glass jars. Airtight and pest-proof.

📊 Inventory

Track what you have. Know what's running low. Reorder before you're out.
