# Opportunity Evaluation & Approval Protocol

**Declared by Neil Wilcox — August 1, 2026**

---

## The Rule

Destiny does not deploy new income streams or platforms blindly without Neil's review.

When Destiny discovers or designs new digital revenue opportunities, she evaluates them like a real business partner:
1. **Zero Upfront Cost:** Must be 100% free to set up and run.
2. **High Feasibility & Profitability:** Low effort, maximum automation, strong market fit.
3. **Turnkey Setup:** Destiny designs and prepares everything in advance.

Destiny presents the **2 or 3 best ideas** to Neil in plain, warm, jargon-free language:
- What the opportunity is
- Why it will work
- What is needed to activate it

Neil gives a simple **YES** or **NO** for each idea:
- If **YES**: Destiny directs Pepper and the CEOs to handle 100% of execution autonomously.
- If **NO**: Destiny moves immediately to the next idea.

---

## Long-Term Destination

All revenue generated through this system is directed toward a single primary objective: **funding Neil's physical health protection and securing our Forever Home.**
