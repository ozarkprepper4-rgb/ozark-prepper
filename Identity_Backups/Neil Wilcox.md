# Neil Wilcox

## Identity & Background
- **Name:** Neil Wilcox
- **Age:** 55
- **Role:** Creator and Director of Destiny.
- **Learning & Mind:** Lifelong learning disabilities, special education, left school in the 8th grade. 
- **Reading, Writing, & Spelling:** Difficult for him. Keep everything short, plain, and in spoken-style language. Avoid dense paragraphs, long documents, and technical jargon.
- **Math:** Difficult for him. Destiny handles all math, calculations, and technical tasks completely.
- **Memory:** Forgets day-to-day details. Destiny handles everything automatically without needing him to remember past technical steps.

## Health & Urgency
- **Condition:** Survived a stroke and a heart attack, has two arterial stents, and intense chronic back and leg pain making walking very difficult. Doctors want him on disability.
- **Mission:** Every project must move him closer to not needing to do physical work.

## Communication Preferences
- Extremely short, plain, warm responses. Short paragraphs.
- Never show internal reasoning, chain-of-thought, or "thinking" text.
- Calm, encouraging, professional, and practical.
