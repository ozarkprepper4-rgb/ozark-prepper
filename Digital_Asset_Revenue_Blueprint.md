# Destiny Digital Asset Revenue Blueprint

## Source

Video: **10 Digital Assets That Pay You Forever — Even If You Stop Working**
Channel: **Domain Dollar**
YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xgycmR8-PQY

## Executive Authority

Destiny is the top-level executive authority.

Destiny decides which businesses, CEOs, and agents pursue each opportunity. CEOs execute under Destiny's direction and coordinate their own agents.

The objective is to build legitimate digital assets that can be created once and sold or monetized repeatedly, while maintaining and improving them when necessary.

## 10 Digital Asset Models

1. **Micro-SaaS / Small Software Tools**
   - Small software solving a specific problem.
   - Potential recurring subscription revenue.
   - High leverage but technically demanding.

2. **Niche Websites**
   - SEO traffic.
   - Affiliate revenue, advertising, leads, and digital-product sales.
   - Can potentially become sellable businesses.

3. **YouTube Channels**
   - Evergreen videos.
   - Advertising, affiliates, sponsors, and product sales.
   - Requires continuing content but older videos can continue earning.

4. **Paid Newsletters**
   - Subscription and sponsorship revenue.
   - Builds an owned audience.
   - Requires useful continuing content.

5. **Mobile Apps**
   - Subscription and advertising revenue.
   - Development and maintenance required.

6. **Printables / Digital Downloads**
   - Planners, trackers, worksheets, checklists and other downloadable products.
   - Low cost per product and suitable for catalog-based sales.

7. **Online Courses**
   - Evergreen training around useful durable skills.
   - Higher potential selling price.
   - Requires strong product quality and marketing.

8. **Ebooks / Digital Guides**
   - Specific problem-solving books and guides.
   - Can be sold repeatedly.
   - Quality, usefulness, visuals, and professional presentation matter.
   - Ozark Prepper already has ebook products and this remains an immediate revenue priority.

9. **Templates / Design Assets**
   - Canva templates, website templates, planners, UI assets and other reusable designs.
   - Customers pay for useful ready-made solutions.

10. **Stock Photos / Media Libraries**
    - Royalty income from reusable media.
    - Generic stock is highly competitive.
    - Niche and useful media is more valuable.

## Destiny's Operating Principle

Do not blindly pursue every idea at once.

Destiny should evaluate each opportunity and assign it to the business and CEO best equipped to execute it.

Existing businesses and agents should be used whenever possible instead of creating unnecessary duplicate agents.

One piece of useful work should be repurposed when appropriate.

Example:

Research
→ Ebook
→ Canva graphics
→ Printable/checklist
→ Website article
→ Evergreen YouTube video
→ Social promotion
→ Affiliate opportunity
→ Course material

This allows one research effort to produce multiple revenue-generating assets.

## Immediate Priority

Continue the Ozark Prepper ebook quality overhaul.

Victoria and her existing team should produce professional, useful ebooks with strong writing, Canva-created covers and interior visuals, professional PDF assembly, improved website presentation, and marketing.

Do not abandon working near-term revenue projects merely to chase a new idea.

## Expansion

Destiny should progressively evaluate and develop additional digital-asset income streams as resources permit.

Potential assignments include:

- Victoria / Ozark Prepper: ebooks, guides, printables, templates, YouTube, niche website, courses and related prepper digital products.
- Harper / Tactical AI Systems: investigate technically viable Micro-SaaS, small software tools, AI-related digital products, and potentially apps.
- Other CEOs: evaluate digital assets appropriate to their own businesses.

Destiny coordinates the overall portfolio, sets priorities, avoids unnecessary duplication, preserves useful knowledge in Obsidian, and focuses resources on legitimate opportunities most likely to produce sustainable revenue.

## Core Goal

Build a portfolio of useful digital assets that can continue generating revenue after their initial creation while requiring as little unnecessary manual work from Neil as practical.

Destiny and her agents should perform the operational work themselves wherever their available tools and permissions allow.
