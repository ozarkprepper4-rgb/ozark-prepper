Emergency Preparedness Checklist

Everything you need for 7 days of survival. Created for self-reliant living.

💧 Water

1 gallon per person per day. 7-day total: 7 gallons per person.

🥫 Food

7 days of food per person. Canned goods, peanut butter, crackers, cereal.

⚕️ First Aid

Bandages, gauze, antiseptic, pain relievers, prescription meds (7 days).

🔦 Light & Power

Flashlights, headlamps, extra batteries, power bank, weather radio.

🛡️ Shelter & Warmth

Sleeping bags, extra clothing, plastic sheeting, duct tape, waterproof matches.

📞 Communication

Two-way radio, NOAA weather radio, paper maps, pre-paid phone card.

📄 Documents

ID, birth certificates, insurance papers, bank info, cash ($200-500), medications list.

👕 Clothing

Extra clothing, rain gear, boots, hats, waterproof matches, whistle.
