# Plans

Neil's Master Strategic Timeline and Phased Development Roadmap, coordinated and tracked by [[Destiny]].

## 🗺️ The Strategy at a Glance
To preserve Neil's physical health, we are moving as fast as possible through a multi-phase digital and real-world execution.

```text
  [ PHASE 1: BRAND REVENUE ]  ──►  [ PHASE 2: INVESTMENT REAL ESTATE ]  ──►  [ THE SANCTUARY ]
    - Ozark Prepper Launch            - NW Investments Launch                   - Secure Off-Grid Homestead
    - Affiliate & Ebook Sales         - Asset Acquisition                       - HavenOS Automation
```

---

## Phase 1: Digital Brand & Immediate Cash Flow (ACTIVE)
* **Objective:** Establish reliable automated digital income.
* **Lead Project:** [[Ozark Prepper]] Brand
* **Key Tasks:**
  - [x] Configure primary brand email: `ozarkprepper4@gmail.com`.
  - [x] Create local **[[HavenOS]]** portal for fast off-grid calculations and content scripting.
  - [x] Create local **[[ClaudeChat]]** standalone playground for clean, fast LLM research.
  - [x] Retrieve, analyze, and implement best practices from the *Agent OS* course framework.
  - [ ] Remind Neil to secure the temporary brand setups with high-entropy passwords.
  - [ ] Write and distribute high-value free prepping/homesteading ebooks.
  - [ ] Set up travel booking affiliate websites to collect booking commission revenues.

## Phase 2: Wealth Multiplication & Strategic Asset Leasing (PENDING PHASE 1)
* **Objective:** Expand digital capital into physical real estate assets.
* **Lead Project:** [[NW Investments]]
* **Key Tasks:**
  - Incorporate the legal entity once steady revenue flows from Phase 1.
  - Buy, sell, lease, and trade tactical or regional property assets.
  - Configure [[Destiny]] to monitor potential real estate deals and zoning alerts 24/7.
  - Set up persistent tax filing ledgers ready for accountant reviews.

## Future Vision: The Sanctuary (LONG-TERM)
* **Objective:** Complete physical security and self-reliance.
* **Lead Project:** [[Off-Grid Bunker and Homestead]]
* **Key Tasks:**
  - Buy a remote plot in "the middle of nowhere" with water access.
  - Deploy a comprehensive solar and battery microgrid designed by [[Sustenance]].
  - Establish HAM radio stations maintained by [[Comms]].
  - Construct physical fabrication shops (plasma, 3D printers, CNC tables) and medical/animal clinics operated by [[HavenOS]].
  - Run cybersecurity audits and local network training labs inside [[Kali Linux VM]].
