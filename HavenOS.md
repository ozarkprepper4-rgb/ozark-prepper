# HavenOS

A local-first, privacy-first "Agent OS" designed specifically for prepping, homesteading, and off-grid living, integrated directly with Neil's home server and Obsidian vault.

```text
                     [ HAVENOS PORTAL (Port 8000) ]
                                    │
          ┌──────────────┬──────────┼──────────┬──────────────┐
          ▼              ▼          ▼          ▼              ▼
     [[Destiny]]    [[Sentinel]] [[Provident]] [[Sustenance]]  [[Comms]]
    (Command AI)   (Hardware/Sec) (Pantry/Res)  (Solar/Agri) (Radio Signals)
```

## Core Architecture & Technical Stack
* **Local Web Interface:** Served on local Port `8000` via a fast, tactical monospace dark-themed UI.
* **Backend Engine:** FastAPI & Python running in `/home/destiny/video_env`.
* **AI Core:** Ollama on Port `11434` executing local open-source models (`qwen2.5:14b` and `hermes3:latest`).
* **Memory Sync:** Directly reads, writes, and displays markdown logs in Neil's active Obsidian Vault (`/home/destiny/Obsidian/Destiny`).
* **Tactical JSON Stores:** Purely offline, FOSS JSON data ledgers located under `/home/destiny/HavenOS/database/`:
  - `inventory.json`: Managed by [[Provident]] for tracking critical stockpiles.
  - `frequencies.json`: Managed by [[Comms]] for RF coordination.
  - `garden.json`: Managed by [[Sustenance]] for planting calendars.
  - `solar.json`: Managed by [[Sustenance]] for battery and solar load calculation.

## The Specialized Agent Team
* 👑 **[[Victoria]] (CEO Agent):** Strategic supervisor, project coordinator, and team manager aligning everyone with Neil's goals.
* 🎙️ **[[Destiny]] (Central Intelligence):** The master coordinator, face/voice of the homestead, and strategic advisor.
* 🛡️ **[[Sentinel]] (Security & Hardware):** Monitors physical server temperatures, drive integrity (RAID), disk free space, CPU/GPU utilization, network firewalls, and orchestrates the [[Kali Linux VM]].
* 🥫 **[[Provident]] (Pantry & Rations):** Computes days-of-survival rations, organizes vacuum-sealed long-term storage, and flags items nearing expiration.
* ☀️ **[[Sustenance]] (Homestead & Solar):** Designs mathematical solar array profiles (battery Ah, load discharge limits) and crop rotation cycles.
* 📡 **[[Comms]] (Radio & Signals):** Logs local repeater CTCSS tones, HF/VHF simplex channels, and coordinates wilderness emergency transmission checklists.
* 🎬 **Marketing Pipeline:** Creative marketing engine composed of [[Audrey]] (Content Research), [[Bridget]] (Copywriting), and [[Chloe]] (SEO Optimization).
