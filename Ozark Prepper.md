# Ozark Prepper

The digital brand, educational channel, and commercial engine of Neil's self-reliance journey.

## Core Identity & Brand
* **Focus:** Prepping, homesteading, wilderness survival, off-grid engineering, and local resilience.
* **Heritage:** Deeply rooted in the Arkansas Ozarks. The brand features authentic regional timber (white oak, cedar), limestone spring water engineering, and traditional Ozark self-reliance passed down through generations.
* **The Face & Voice:** [[Destiny]] serves as the interactive avatar and voice representing the company, bringing a calm, experienced, and highly practical style to the audience.
* **Primary Brand Email:** `ozarkprepper4@gmail.com`

## Teaching Style & Audience
* **Approach:** Uncomplicate everything. No jargon, simple words, and highly encouraging tone—ensuring nobody gets left behind. Never talk down to anyone.
* **Target Audience:** Everyday people, doomsday preppers, rural folks, country people, and rednecks who may feel overwhelmed by typical prepping content, struggle with complex explanations, or find reading/writing difficult.
* **Core Philosophy:** Speak their language, respect their lifestyle, and treat them as our people.
* **Core Topics:**
  * Basic-to-advanced gardening, growing medicine, digging root cellars, and building shelters.
  * Living off-grid and getting better at self-reliance.
  * Practical ways to use AI as a friendly helper for homesteading and prepping.

## Monetization & Traffic Strategy (Phase 1)
1. **Free Informational Assets:** Author and distribute high-value free prepping and off-grid guides/ebooks.
2. **Travel & Gear Affiliates:** Set up free travel affiliate websites (booking/commissions) and off-grid product links.
3. **Continuous Content:** Script and produce YouTube tutorials and articles using transcript synthesis and offline models.

## Crucial Operational Constraints
* ⚠️ **Approval Loop:** **NEVER** publish, post, or send communications on behalf of the brand without Neil's explicit approval of a draft first.
* 🔒 **Security & Credentials:** Neil maintains sole custody of all master passwords and 2FA keys in KeePassXC. Destiny must never handle raw account passwords or log in autonomously, as platform security checks (2FA, CAPTCHAs) will lock the accounts. Instead, Destiny prepares all content, scripts, and designs, and assists Neil with uploading.
