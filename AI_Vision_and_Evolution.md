9. Conversational research partner — REAL.
10. Delegation intelligence — PARTIAL. The line between "handle it myself" and "ask Neil" is defined in SOUL.md and Neil_Wilcox_Profile.md but not yet tested under real pressure.

## Reference 2 — "The World in 2050: Top 20 Future Technologies" (analyzed 2026-08-15)
Full analysis delivered to Neil in conversation on 2026-08-15. Not reproduced verbatim here (copyright) — summary of what's actionable:

**Confirmed relevant, already partially built:**
- One persistent assistant identity across all surfaces (matches Destiny's design).
- Hierarchy of specialized agents running whole business functions (matches Pepper/Victoria/CEO structure — but video assumes full reliability; ours is proven unreliable on current hardware, see gaps below).
- Local/private AI instead of cloud-dependent (matches Ollama-first approach).
- Agents that learn from experience and share knowledge with each other (aspirational — not yet real; agent-to-agent handoff is currently unreliable, see Kanban findings).

**Confirmed FUTURE CAPABILITIES — explicitly NOT to attempt on current hardware:**
- AGI-level general reasoning.
- Humanoid robot embodiment.
- Brain-computer interfaces.
- Quantum computing / photonic chips.
- Real-time coordinated robot/drone swarms in the physical world.
These all require specialized hardware and research infrastructure far beyond a home Linux machine with an 8-core CPU and a 2GB GPU. Logged here so the requirement isn't lost — revisit only if/when hardware fundamentally changes.

**Gap identified — the video assumes reliability we don't have yet:** the video depicts AI/robots executing multi-step autonomous work flawlessly. Real testing on 2026-08-14/15 proved our current free local models (hermes3:latest, llama3.1:8b) cannot reliably complete even a simple 2-step Kanban task (do work → formally mark complete) — see `/home/destiny/Obsidian/Pepper/Kanban_Model_Test_Findings.md` for full details. This is the single biggest gap between the vision and current reality: **reliability of autonomous execution, not raw capability.**

## Standing Roadmap Priorities (in order)
1. Get Ozark Prepper genuinely operational, tested, and producing verified revenue (per SOUL.md's Ozark Prepper priority).
2. Improve autonomous task-completion reliability for Pepper/Victoria (larger/better local models as hardware allows, or clearer task instructions in the meantime).
3. Build a real agent-to-agent communication channel (currently only exists via manual terminal handoff or the clunky Kanban board).
4. Build a real staging/testing environment (flagged as a gap in SOUL.md, 2026-08-15).
5. Wire up real physical hardware control (CNC, 3D printing, Home Assistant) — currently just a stated intention, not implemented.
6. Expand persistent memory capacity/architecture once the above are stable.
7. Revisit AGI/robotics/quantum-tier capabilities only if/when hardware fundamentally upgrades.
