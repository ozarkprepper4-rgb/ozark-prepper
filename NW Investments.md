# NW Investments

The financial vehicle designed for Neil's Phase 2 expansion and long-term asset building.

## 💼 Core Business Directive
* **Identity:** NW Investments (Phase 2 of [[Plans]]).
* **Function:** Real estate investment, property acquisition, leasing, and strategic capital allocation.
* **Ultimate Goal:** Build robust, non-physical revenue channels to fully fund the construction of [[Off-Grid Bunker and Homestead]].

## 📊 Operations & Strategic Rules
1. **Pacing:** Executed only after [[Ozark Prepper]] (Phase 1) has successfully established a consistent, steady cash flow.
2. **Analysis:** Let [[Destiny]] monitor target real estate listings, zoning boards, and foreclosure registries 24/7 to flag deals.
3. **Credit Management:** Leverage strategic credit lines and cards to build corporate scorecards, while finding the absolute cheapest capital rates available.
4. **Accounting Rigor:** Keep personal and corporate banking strictly segregated, maintaining complete ledger trails for seamless CPA verification.
