Cheap Airlines vs Cheap Flights: How to Pick

Two words people use for the same thing: flights and airfare. But they are not the same, and knowing the difference saves you money.

Cheap airfare is the ticket price you see advertised. Cheap flights is the whole trip — the ticket plus bags, seat, meals, and whether you can actually get to the airport without a nightmare.

What is cheaper

  • Cheap airfare looks lower. But it often hides fees for carry-on, checked bags, seat selection, and even the gate you stand in line at.
  • Cheap flights means the real cost you pay at the end. A $300 ticket with a $60 carry-on and a $40 seat is not cheap.

How to pick

  • Add up every fee before you decide.
  • If you travel with a bag, pick the airline that gives your bag free.
  • If you want to sit where you like, add that to the cost.
  • If you change your mind, check the change fee.
