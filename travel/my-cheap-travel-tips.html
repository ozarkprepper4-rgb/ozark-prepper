My Cheap Travel Tips: What I Learned

These are the tips that saved me the most money on my travels. They are plain and proven.

  1. Bring your own water bottle. Fill it at the airport before you fly. Many airports have water fountains.
  2. Pack a small snacks bag. Airport food is expensive. A sandwich and water cost less and taste better.
  3. Check the airline's website directly. Sometimes the airline's own site has the best price and the best refund policy.
  4. Keep your boarding pass on your phone. Print a backup. Do not lose it.
  5. Check in online early. It can save you from airport lines and sometimes from checked-bag fees.
  6. Use a travel backpack. One bag is easier to track than two or three.
  7. Read the fine print. Check the refund policy before you buy.
  8. Share your trip with family. If something goes wrong, they know where you are.
