How to Find Cheap Flights: A Plain-English Guide

Finding cheap flights is a skill. You build it one search at a time. Here is how.

Step 1: Open three or more search tabs. Google Flights, Expedia, the airline site.

Step 2: Search for your dates. Look at the price for each. Write them down.

Step 3: Change your dates by one day. Sometimes one day makes a big difference.

Step 4: Change your airport. A nearby airport can be cheaper.

Step 5: Clear your shopping cookies. Some sites raise prices after you look.

Step 6: Use a price alert. Get an email when the price drops.

Step 7: Fly mid-week. Tuesday and Wednesday are often cheapest.

Step 8: Fly at dawn or late night. These seats are often cheapest.

Step 9: Look at budget airlines. They often cost less, but charge for bags.

Step 10: Book when you are sure. Do not rush.
