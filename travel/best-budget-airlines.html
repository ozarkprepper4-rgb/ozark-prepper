Best Budget Airlines for Older Travelers

Written by a person who has flown cheap most of her life. Plain words. No jargon.

You do not need a lot of money to fly. You need a plan. This page tells you which airlines are easiest and cheapest to fly, especially if you travel with luggage, a walker, or a little extra care.

The short list

  1. Southwest — no carry-on fee, no seat assignment fee. Easiest for your bags.
  2. Frontier — cheapest fares, but charges for carry-on. Only pick this if you travel light.
  3. Allegiant — cheap, but no free changes or free carry-on. Read the rules first.
  4. United, American, Delta — not always cheapest, but have the most flexible options.

How to find the cheapest seat

  • Search on more than one site. Google Flights and Expedia both cost the same to you.
  • Fly mid-week. Tuesday and Wednesday are often cheaper.
  • Fly at dawn or late night. These seats are often cheaper.
  • Set a price alert. Many sites let you do this free.

What to read before you buy

  • Check the carry-on size and weight. Cheap airlines often charge for your bag.
  • Check the seat size and legroom if you want more room.
  • Check the change fee before you book.
  • Check if you need travel insurance. Sometimes it is worth it; sometimes not.

My honest pick for you

If you want the least stress and one bag is free: Southwest. If you want the absolute lowest price and you travel with only a personal item: Frontier or Allegiant.
