# 🎨 Obsidian Graph Color-Coding & Graphify Setup Guide

This guide explains how to customize your **Obsidian Graph View** with beautiful colors matching your note types, and how to use **Graphify** (our free and open-source AI knowledge mapping engine).

---

## 🌈 Part 1: How to Color-Code Your Obsidian Graph (Native & Free)

Obsidian has a built-in, local-first Graph Grouping engine that lets you color-code your notes dynamically based on directories, tags, or search queries. 

### Step-by-Step Color Setup:
1. Open your Obsidian application and open the Graph View (`Ctrl + G`).
2. Click the **Gear (Settings)** icon in the top-right corner of the Graph panel.
3. Expand the **Groups** section and click **New Group**.
4. Create the following groups to color-code your notes. Type the query in the input field, then pick the suggested HEX color:

#### 🟢 1. Ozark Prepper Assets (Forest Green)
- **Query:** `path:"Ozark Prepper"`
- **Color:** `#2ecc71` or `#27ae60` (Green)
- *Applies to:* All survival guides, video scripts, marketing packs, and status logs.

#### 🔵 2. Destiny Core Identity (Deep Blue)
- **Query:** `path:"Destiny"`
- **Color:** `#3498db` or `#2980b9` (Blue)
- *Applies to:* Your AI companion files, system configs, plans, and sub-agent blueprints.

#### 🔴 3. Security & Secure Vault (Crimson Red)
- **Query:** `file:Secure_Vault_Setup` or `file:init_secure_vault`
- **Color:** `#e74c3c` or `#c0392b` (Red)
- *Applies to:* Cryptographic configurations, local vault setup guides, and secure access protocols.

#### 🟣 4. General Goals & Self Improvement (Amethyst Purple)
- **Query:** `file:"My Goals"` or `file:"Self Improvement"`
- **Color:** `#9b59b6` (Purple)
- *Applies to:* Life goals, fitness, learning benchmarks, and long-term targets.

Once added, these colors instantly update across your Graph View, making your note relationships visually clear!

---

## 🚀 Part 2: Graphify Knowledge Graph Engine (FOSS)

We have successfully installed **Graphify**, a free, open-source AI coding assistant skill and knowledge graphing tool. Graphify maps files, directories, and documents into highly detailed interactive graphs, including markdown files, PDFs, and codebase structures.

### How to use Graphify locally on your vault:
To compile your entire Obsidian workspace into an interactive 3D/2D HTML graph, run this command in your terminal:
```bash
/home/destiny/video_env/bin/graphify tree --root /home/destiny/Obsidian --output /home/destiny/Obsidian/graphify_interactive.html
```

### Features of Graphify:
- **Interactive Tree & Canvas:** Generates `graphify_interactive.html` which you can open in any browser to click nodes, view connections, search, and navigate.
- **Hermes Integration:** Graphify is fully installed as a Hermes system skill under your active profile (`~/.hermes/skills/graphify/`).
