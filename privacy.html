Privacy Policy

Short version: we don't track you, and we don't sell your data. Ever.

What We Collect

  • This website itself collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking cookies, no ad networks, no sign-up forms. The pages are static files.
  • Payments: when you buy an ebook, Stripe processes the payment. Stripe collects your email and payment details under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). We receive your email and what you bought — we never see your full card number.
  • Email: if you write to us, we get your email address and use it only to reply or handle your order and refunds.

What We Never Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Not to anyone, not ever.
  • We do not add you to mailing lists you didn't ask for.

Third-Party Links

Affiliate links take you to other stores (Amazon, TravelPayouts, Signature Solar, and others). Those sites have their own privacy practices; read their policies before buying.

How AI Is Used Here

Honesty matters to us: our ebooks and articles are written with the help of AI tools and reviewed by a human editor before publication. We cite sources where it matters (weather, food safety, power safety) and we don't invent facts, reviews, or testimonials.

Questions Or Deletions

Want your purchase record deleted? Email ozarkprepper4@gmail.com and we'll remove what we can (we keep what tax law requires us to keep, like proof of sale).
