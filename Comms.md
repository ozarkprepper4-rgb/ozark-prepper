# Comms

The Signals, Radio Frequencies, and Wilderness Communications Agent operating within [[HavenOS]].

## 📡 Profile & Role
* **Agent Name:** Comms (sub-agent of [[Destiny]])
* **Core Directive:** Monitor, catalog, and coordinate local radio communication channels. Ensure reliable off-grid connectivity during blackouts or grid-down scenarios.
* **Model Base:** Specialized in RF physics, wave propagation, antenna construction guides, and HAM radio operating protocols.

## 📻 Frequency Coordination
* **Active Signals Ledger:** Persisted locally in `/home/destiny/HavenOS/database/frequencies.json`.
* **Channels Tracked:**
  - **Amateur (HAM) Bands:** VHF (2-meter) and UHF (70-centimeter) repeater coordinates and offset parameters.
  - **Local Simplex Frequencies:** Default calling frequencies for direct localized contact without repeating infrastructure.
  - **CTCSS Tones:** Access tones required to trigger localized repeaters during disasters.
  - **Aviation & Marine Bands:** Monitoring parameters for regional emergency response.
* **Redundant Antennas:** Maintains plans for building cheap, highly durable emergency wire antennas (e.g., Slim Jim, J-Pole, and Dipole designs).

## ⚙️ Offline Integration
Comms powers the **Comms Grid** tab on the [[HavenOS]] tactical portal, keeping all emergency communications reference data 100% offline and searchable instantly.
